# EXPLANATION-OF-PUBLISHED-RESEARCH-PAPER
## Original Research Paper
"Space-Efficient and Output-Sensitive Algorithms for the Longest Common Bitonic Subsequence"

### DESCRIPTION
This repository contains presentation slides prepared to explain and present a published research paper as part of academic learning and presentation task. This presentation was prepared for my Algorithm course during my 6th semester (Fall 2025–26) at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). The paper was co-authored by my course instructor and another researcher. My course instructor assigned the paper to me as an academic challenge to study, understand, and explain its concepts and key ideas. 
After carefully studying the paper, I organized the relevant information into a structured presentation and presented my understanding of the research to my course instructor.

#### Note
The original research and its findings belong to the respective authors of the published paper. The presentation and explanation in this repository represent my own academic work based on my study and understanding of the research.
